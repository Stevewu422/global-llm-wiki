# nashsu/llm_wiki

`nashsu/llm_wiki` turns the [[llm-wiki-pattern]] into a full desktop application.
Instead of only shipping prompts or skills, it adds UI, ingest queueing, graph views,
local APIs, an MCP server, and bundled agent-skill integration.^[raw/articles/2026-06-25-llm-wiki-landscape.md]

Its most notable addition is productization: the wiki becomes a software system that
humans and agents can both use directly.^[raw/articles/2026-06-25-llm-wiki-landscape.md]

Compared with [[kfchou-wiki-skills]] and [[lewislulu-llm-wiki-skill]], it is heavier
but closer to an end-state product.
