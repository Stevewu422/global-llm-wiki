# Wiki Purpose

## Why This Wiki Exists

This wiki is the durable memory layer for INPAY's local AI operating system.
It exists to reduce repeated rediscovery across Codex, Hermes, OpenClaw, local
skills, automation runs, research notes, and operating procedures.

## Core Goals

- Turn scattered notes, repo files, and external research into reusable knowledge.
- Make future agent sessions faster, safer, and less dependent on chat history.
- Preserve high-signal implementation patterns for skills, operations, and tooling.
- Support human review with explicit source traces and lightweight lint checks.

## Key Questions

- Which LLM Wiki implementation patterns are most reusable for INPAY?
- How should repo-local wiki maintenance fit Codex/Hermes/OpenClaw workflows?
- Which pieces belong in raw sources, structured wiki pages, or executable scripts?
- How do we keep knowledge current without turning the wiki into a pile of duplicates?

## Current Scope

- Karpathy-style LLM Wiki patterns
- External implementations: `wiki-skills`, `pin-llm-wiki`, `llm-wiki-skill`, `llm_wiki`
- INPAY-specific local knowledge workflow design

## Non-Goals

- This is not a production backend.
- This is not a fully autonomous publishing pipeline.
- This wiki should not hold secrets, credentials, or private server details.
