# lewislulu/llm-wiki-skill

`lewislulu/llm-wiki-skill` is the implementation most directly aligned with
OpenClaw/Codex usage. It combines the [[llm-wiki-pattern]] with audit and review
tooling, including an Obsidian audit plugin and a local web viewer.^[raw/articles/2026-06-25-llm-wiki-landscape.md]

Its main contribution is the feedback loop: the wiki is not only generated, but also
reviewed, commented on, and corrected through dedicated tooling.^[raw/articles/2026-06-25-llm-wiki-landscape.md]

Compared with [[nashsu-llm-wiki]], it stays closer to a skill-first model. Compared
with [[kfchou-wiki-skills]], it is more opinionated about review ergonomics.
